Delete Your Account

If you no longer want to use Pick & Go Now, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Pick & Go Now account, follow these steps:

1
Send us a request Contact our support team using the email address provided below.
2
Provide your account details Include the email address associated with your Pick & Go Now account so we can identify your account.
3
Account verification We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request belongs to the account owner.
4
Deletion is processed After verification, we will process the account deletion request in accordance with our policies and applicable requirements.

What happens to your data?

When your account deletion request is processed, personal information associated with your account will be deleted or anonymized where applicable.

  • Account profile information
  • Account authentication information
  • Profile images associated with your account
  • User-provided information stored in your account
Information that may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, accounting, or other legitimate purposes. Such information will be retained only for the applicable period.

Before requesting deletion

Account deletion may affect your access to Pick & Go Now services, including your account history and other account-related information.

If you have an unresolved order, payment issue, dispute, or other active matter, please mention it in your deletion request.

Request account deletion

Contact the Pick & Go Now support team to submit your account deletion request.
