Your Privacy Matters Pick & Go Now is committed to protecting your personal information and using it responsibly to provide food ordering, hotel, account, notification, and related services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pick & Go Now ("Pick & Go Now", "we", "us", or "our").

Pick & Go Now is a food ordering and related service platform that allows customers to discover participating hotels and food products, place orders, receive order updates, and manage their accounts.

The platform may also allow approved hotel owners to manage their hotel information, food categories, products, availability, and customer orders.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the Pick & Go Now application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Pick & Go Now, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

Account Information

Name, email address, phone number, profile information, and authentication data.

Order Information

Products, quantities, prices, hotel information, order status, notes, and related order details.

Images & Documents

Images and documents that you voluntarily upload through supported features.

Technical Information

Application version, device information, crash information, and technical diagnostics.

3. Account Information

When you create or use a Pick & Go Now account, we may process information necessary to create, authenticate, and maintain your account.

Customer Accounts

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Profile image, where provided
  • Authentication information

Hotel Owner Accounts

Approved or applying hotel owners may additionally provide information related to their hotel or business, including:

  • Hotel name
  • Business contact information
  • Hotel address
  • Hotel description
  • Hotel images
  • Application information
  • Supporting documents, where required

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, Google may provide us with information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address, and profile image, according to the authorization you provide.

4. Order Information

When you place an order through Pick & Go Now, we may process information necessary to create, manage, fulfill, and track the order.

This may include:

  • Selected hotel or food provider
  • Selected products
  • Product quantities
  • Product prices
  • Order totals
  • Hotel-specific checkout notes
  • Order status
  • Order timestamps
  • Order history

Where an order involves multiple participating hotels, information necessary to fulfill each part of the order may be provided to the relevant hotel or service provider.

5. Images & Uploaded Files

Pick & Go Now may allow users to select or upload images and files for specific application features.

Customers

  • Profile images
  • Other images voluntarily submitted through supported features

Hotel Owners

  • Hotel images
  • Product images
  • Profile images
  • Application documents, where required

We do not intend to access your device's photo library beyond what is necessary for the image-selection or upload feature you choose to use.

You should only upload files that you have the right to provide and that are necessary for the relevant application feature.

6. Location Information

Pick & Go Now may use location information only if a location-based feature is enabled in the application.

If the application requests location permission, the permission will be requested through the operating system and you may control the permission through your device settings.

If location information is not required by a particular version of the application, we do not intentionally collect device location for that version.

7. Push Notifications

Pick & Go Now may use push notifications to provide timely information about your account, orders, applications, and other relevant activity.

Notifications may include:

  • Order creation notifications
  • Order status updates
  • Hotel-owner order notifications
  • Hotel application status notifications
  • Important account or service notifications

Push notifications may be delivered using Firebase Cloud Messaging or another appropriate notification service.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

8. Firebase Services

Pick & Go Now uses Google Firebase services to support parts of the application's functionality.

Depending on the features enabled in the application, Firebase services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Storage
  • Firebase Cloud Functions
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services may process information necessary to authenticate users, store application data, store uploaded files, send notifications, execute backend operations, and diagnose technical problems.

Firebase services are provided by Google and may process information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

9. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Pick & Go Now for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Processing food and product orders
  • Managing hotel-owner applications
  • Managing hotel and product information
  • Sending order notifications
  • Sending application status notifications
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining application security
  • Preventing unauthorized activity
  • Detecting technical errors
  • Improving application performance
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

10. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide the services you request.

Customers

Information necessary to fulfill an order may be made available to the relevant participating hotel or food provider.

Hotel Owners

Information related to orders may be made available to the relevant hotel owner for fulfilling and managing those orders.

Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers, including infrastructure and technology providers, to operate the application.

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Pick & Go Now.

Security measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, Firebase security rules, backend authorization, encrypted network communication, and appropriate application security practices.

However, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and should notify us if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain legitimate business records, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and prevent fraud or abuse.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize, or otherwise securely dispose of it where appropriate.

13. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Pick & Go Now account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the email address provided in the Contact Us section of this Privacy Policy.

When we receive a valid deletion request, we will review and process it in accordance with applicable requirements and our legitimate operational needs.

Certain information may need to be retained when required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, protect security, or comply with legitimate record-keeping requirements.

14. Children's Privacy
